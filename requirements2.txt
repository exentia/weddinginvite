# Interactive Wedding Invitation Experience – Envelope and Event Cards

I want the wedding invitation website to have a unique interactive experience that resembles opening a physical wedding invitation envelope containing multiple invitation cards.

This is one of the most important parts of the website. Please pay special attention to the animation, realism, interaction flow, and visual quality.

## 1. Initial Screen – Closed Wedding Envelope

When a guest first visits the website, they should not immediately see a traditional website layout.

Instead, the entire first screen should display a beautifully designed wedding invitation envelope.

### Envelope Design

The envelope should look luxurious and elegant, inspired by premium physical wedding invitations.

Design elements:

- Premium textured paper appearance.
- Deep emerald green, ivory, champagne gold, or colors inspired by the reference video.
- Elegant Islamic geometric patterns.
- Gold decorative borders.
- Subtle floral ornaments if appropriate.
- Beautiful envelope flap.
- Elegant wax seal or decorative emblem.
- Soft shadows to create depth.
- Realistic paper texture.
- Sophisticated lighting.

The envelope should be centered on the screen and should look like a physical object.

### Text on the Envelope

The envelope should contain the following:

**Arabic Bismillah:**

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

**English text:**

With the blessings of Allah

**Bride and Groom Names:**

[BRIDE NAME]

&

[GROOM NAME]

The names should be elegantly displayed using premium typography.

The text should be positioned carefully so that it looks like it is printed on the envelope itself.

### Interaction

The envelope should invite the guest to open it.

Add a subtle interaction hint such as:

"Tap to open your invitation"

or

"Open Invitation"

The envelope should have a subtle animation, such as gentle floating, a soft glow, or a slight movement, to indicate that it is interactive.

When the user clicks or taps anywhere on the envelope, trigger the envelope-opening animation.

---

# 2. Envelope Opening Animation

When the user clicks the envelope:

The envelope flap should open realistically.

The animation should feel like a physical envelope being opened.

Suggested animation sequence:

1. The envelope slightly lifts or moves forward.
2. The wax seal or decorative element disappears or breaks away elegantly if applicable.
3. The envelope flap rotates open.
4. The inside of the envelope becomes visible.
5. Invitation cards inside the envelope become visible.
6. The cards appear stacked inside the envelope.

Use CSS 3D transforms, perspective, rotations, shadows, and layered elements to create a realistic effect.

The animation should be smooth and cinematic.

Avoid simple fade-in effects for the entire envelope.

The envelope should feel like a real physical object opening.

Once the animation completes, the guest should be able to see the invitation cards inside the envelope.

---

# 3. Event Cards Inside the Envelope

The envelope should contain multiple invitation cards, stacked on top of one another.

Each card represents a separate wedding event.

For example:

- Mehndi
- Nikah
- Walima

The guest should be able to identify each event card before opening it.

### Card Design

Each card should have its own unique design while maintaining a consistent wedding invitation theme.

Cards may differ in:

- Decorative patterns
- Accent colors
- Floral elements
- Icons
- Typography
- Borders
- Event-specific ornaments

For example:

**MEHNDI**

A floral and festive design with green and gold elements.

**NIKAH**

A more elegant, spiritual, minimal design with ivory and gold.

**WALIMA**

A sophisticated celebration design with emerald, champagne, and elegant floral elements.

The cards should be visibly stacked, with each card slightly offset from the others so guests can see that multiple invitations are present.

The event name should be clearly visible on each card.

The guest should not need to guess which card represents which event.

---

# 4. Selecting an Event Card

When the guest clicks on a specific event card, that card should automatically animate out of the envelope.

The animation should resemble physically removing a card from a stack.

### Desired Animation

When a card is selected:

1. The selected card moves slightly upward.
2. The card moves forward toward the viewer.
3. The card rotates subtly to create a realistic 3D effect.
4. The card slides out of the envelope.
5. The card becomes the primary focus of the screen.
6. The other cards remain inside the envelope, or move slightly backward to provide depth.
7. The selected card smoothly transitions into the event details view.

Use:

- CSS perspective
- `transform`
- `translate3d`
- Rotation
- Layered shadows
- Smooth easing
- Scale transitions
- Z-index manipulation

The animation should feel like a physical invitation card being pulled out of an envelope.

Do not simply replace the card with a different HTML section instantly.

---

# 5. Event Details Card

After the selected invitation card comes out of the envelope, display the complete details of that event.

The event details should be displayed on the card itself or through an elegant card expansion animation.

For example, the Nikah card could expand into a larger invitation card displaying:

### NIKAH

With the blessings of Allah SWT and our beloved families, we invite you to join us for the Nikah ceremony of:

[BRIDE NAME]

&

[GROOM NAME]

**Date:** [DATE]

**Time:** [TIME]

**Venue:** [VENUE NAME]

**Address:** [FULL ADDRESS]

The design should feel like reading a beautifully printed wedding invitation card.

The card should have:

- Elegant typography
- Event-specific decorations
- Beautiful spacing
- Islamic geometric patterns
- Decorative borders
- Gold accents
- Appropriate event imagery or illustrations if needed

Do not make the details look like a generic website form or information panel.

---

# 6. Add to Calendar Button

At the bottom of each event invitation card, include a clearly visible:

**Add to Calendar**

button.

When clicked, allow the guest to add the event to their calendar.

Implement functionality to generate an `.ics` calendar file compatible with:

- Google Calendar
- Apple Calendar
- Outlook
- Other calendar applications

The calendar event should include:

- Event title
- Event date
- Start time
- End time
- Venue
- Address
- Description

If possible, also provide a Google Calendar link.

The calendar functionality should use the specific details of the selected event.

Each event should have its own calendar entry.

---

# 7. Returning to the Envelope

After the guest finishes viewing an event, provide a beautifully designed navigation control:

"Back to Invitation"

or

"View Other Events"

When the guest clicks this button:

1. The event card should smoothly animate back into the envelope.
2. The envelope should return to its opened state.
3. The remaining invitation cards should be visible.
4. The guest should be able to select another event.

The interaction should feel like returning a physical invitation card into the envelope.

The guest should be able to view all events individually.

---

# 8. Sequential Event Experience

The overall experience should work like this:

### Step 1

Guest opens website.

They see a closed envelope containing:

- Bismillah
- With the blessings of Allah
- Bride and groom names

### Step 2

Guest clicks the envelope.

The envelope opens with a beautiful animation.

### Step 3

Guest sees the stacked invitation cards.

For example:

- Mehndi
- Nikah
- Walima

### Step 4

Guest clicks the Mehndi card.

The Mehndi card slides out of the envelope and reveals its details.

### Step 5

Guest reads the details and clicks Add to Calendar.

### Step 6

Guest clicks Back to Invitation.

The card returns to the envelope.

### Step 7

Guest clicks the Nikah card.

The Nikah card slides out and reveals its details.

### Step 8

Guest adds the Nikah event to their calendar.

### Step 9

Guest returns to the envelope and selects the Walima card.

The same interaction repeats.

### Step 10

After all events have been viewed, the guest can continue to other invitation features, such as:

- RSVP
- Wedding gallery
- Venue location
- Countdown
- Personal message or dua

---

# 9. Animation Quality

The animation is extremely important.

I want the experience to feel like interacting with a real physical wedding invitation.

Use:

- Realistic envelope flap rotation
- 3D perspective
- Layered paper cards
- Natural card movement
- Smooth transitions
- Soft shadows
- Subtle paper movement
- Elegant easing curves
- Appropriate animation timing

Avoid:

- Abrupt transitions
- Simple fade-only animations
- Cartoon-style animations
- Excessive bounce
- Overly fast movement
- Cheap-looking transitions

The animation should be elegant, smooth, realistic, and premium.

---

# 10. Mobile Experience

This interaction must work exceptionally well on mobile devices.

Most guests will open the invitation on their mobile phones.

On mobile:

- The envelope should fit comfortably within the screen.
- The cards should be easy to tap.
- The event details should be readable.
- Animations should remain smooth.
- No horizontal scrolling should occur.
- Buttons should have sufficient touch area.
- The envelope and cards should scale proportionally.

The interaction should support both mouse clicks and touch gestures.

---

---

# 12. Final Expectations

This interaction should be the centerpiece of the wedding invitation website.

I do not want the envelope to merely be a decorative illustration.

I want the envelope and invitation cards to be interactive objects with realistic animations.

The guest should feel that they are:

1. Receiving a physical invitation.
2. Opening the envelope.
3. Discovering the invitation cards.
4. Selecting a specific event.
5. Physically pulling the card out.
6. Reading the invitation.
7. Adding the event to their calendar.
8. Returning the card.
9. Exploring the remaining invitations.

Please prioritize the quality of this experience above adding unnecessary website features.

The finished implementation should look and feel like a premium, custom-designed digital Muslim wedding invitation.
